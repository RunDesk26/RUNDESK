      How RunDesk Check-in works

      RunDesk Check-in is the door scanner for orgs that run on RunDesk. Every check-in saves straight to your org's Google Sheet, in the Attendance file.

      Signing in

      Use the Org Code and password your org received. The password is the Scanner PIN in your sheet's Settings tab, and officers can change it there anytime.

      At the event

      1. Open the event, or create it with New event.
      2. Allow the camera, then scan each member's QR from their welcome email.
      3. Green means checked in. Amber means they already checked in. Red means the QR isn't valid for your org.

      Forgot their QR? Use "Check in by Member ID or student no." under the result.

      After the event

      Turn scanning off for that event so nobody checks in late. Attendance totals are in your sheet under Attendance › Refresh attendance summary.

      Install it like an app

      Android: open the browser menu and tap Install app or Add to Home screen. iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
